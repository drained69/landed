Your balance says five thousand twenty-four fifty. It says nothing about rent
and internet due in days, the groceries this week, or the hundred thirty-four

02 · THE PROBLEM                                    0:17 (slide on at 0:17)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Most money apps stop there. They quote you, then forget what happened: a fee
that doubled, a delivery that took a day, a salary late. And the same mistake
repeats. LANDED replaces the number with an answer, and it learns from every
outcome.

On screen (memory ledger, confirmed lessons): "FastRemit charged $14.80 against
a $0.99 quote" · "FastRemit delivered in 31 hours despite a 4-hour promise" ·
"Salary has arrived late twice in the last four months, once after rent was
due."

03 · THE ANSWER                                     0:34 (slide on at 0:35)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LANDED answers one question: what is safe to move right now? The next
forty-five days are simulated in integer cents: every bill, every savings
contribution, your spending pace, a buffer. Expected income stays out until it
lands. Not five thousand. Not three hundred. One hundred thirty dollars.

On screen: safe to move $130.00, after $1,680.00 protected / $983.78 near-term /
$134.70 buffer.
